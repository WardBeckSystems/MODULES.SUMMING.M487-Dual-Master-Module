## outputs on Ward Beck 74066

_WBS TECHNICAL SUPPORT · Jun 15, 2007|40_

> I've been playing with the calibration of the console latly. It's been a fun endever and has ended in some heated discussion with some of the engineers at the studio. 
>
> so WBS is factory defaut +8, but SOMETIMES there are -4dBu pads on CERTAIN outputs... 
>
> I opened up my M477, took put the 8-109E daughter board and changed the R15 resistor to a 10K multi turn pot. this resistor value allowed me to reduce the output of the M477 from +8 to operate at +4, I dont remember exctly what the resistor ended up being, but to calibrate it, I had to set the faders to -15 (as that is the reference position for the faders) 
>
> if you were to open up your M487 modules (http://wbsps.kuzub.com/PDFs/M487%20Dual%20Master%20Module.pdf) it contains a 8-109C module... it has the same R15... put a pot in, adjust the gain downb by 4dBu and voila... you're now at +4
>
> Tones
